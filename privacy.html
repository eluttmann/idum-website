Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how the International Dialogue on Underwater Munitions (IDUM) handles personal information when you visit idum.org, use its contact form, or contact IDUM about its work. IDUM handles personal information in accordance with applicable Canadian privacy laws, including the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies. Other provincial privacy laws may also apply.

Information we collect

If you use the contact form, IDUM receives the information you provide: your name, email address, message, and inquiry type. Depending on the inquiry, you may also provide your organization, phone number, location, or timeframe. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

If you email or otherwise contact IDUM, IDUM receives your contact details and the contents of your message, including any information you choose to include. The website’s hosting and external resource providers may also process technical connection information such as your IP address, browser, device, requested page, and request time to deliver and protect the site.

How we use information

IDUM uses inquiry information to read and respond to your request, coordinate relevant follow-up, and maintain records of its communications. Technical information may be used by service providers to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website. IDUM does not use contact form information for advertising and does not sell or rent it.

Contact form provider and other services

The contact form is delivered through Web3Forms. Web3Forms processes form submissions on IDUM’s behalf and transmits them to IDUM by email. Web3Forms states that form submissions may be processed through infrastructure providers in multiple regions, and that IP addresses and email addresses may be sent to its spam-prevention providers. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for its current processing, retention, and service-provider details. Those providers may process information outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

The site also loads the Public Sans font from Google Fonts. It uses the Lucide icon library from unpkg on the main page. When your browser requests these resources, the respective providers may receive technical connection information. IDUM does not control those providers’ privacy practices.

Consent and your choices

Providing information through the contact form is voluntary. You may choose not to provide optional details, though IDUM may need enough information to respond. IDUM obtains consent for collection, use, and disclosure where required by applicable law. You may withdraw consent for future handling by contacting IDUM; this does not affect processing already carried out or information IDUM must retain to meet legal obligations or resolve a matter.

Retention and safeguards

IDUM keeps inquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage its relationship with you, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal obligations. The service provider may retain submissions under its own published retention terms. IDUM uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information it handles, and limits access to people who need it for IDUM’s work.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may ask to access personal information IDUM holds about you, correct inaccurate information, or withdraw consent. Send a request to the contact below and describe the information and request as clearly as you can. IDUM may need to verify your identity and may be unable to provide or delete some information where an exception or legal retention requirement applies.

If you are not satisfied with IDUM’s response, you may raise a concern with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the privacy regulator in your province, as applicable. The Commissioner’s guidelines for meaningful consent provide further information about Canadian privacy practices.

Contact IDUM

For a privacy question or request, email oceanbombhunters@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

Updates to this policy

IDUM may update this policy when its practices or legal obligations change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
