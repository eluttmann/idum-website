# Cooperating Organizations — sourced logos

All 20 files in this folder were downloaded at full native resolution from
IDUM's own live site, `underwatermunitions.org/cooperating-organizations-2/`
and `underwatermunitions.org/daimon/` (Polish Naval Academy crest), on
2026-09-30. They are IDUM's own material — no third-party rights question.

This covers 20 of the ~35 organizations on that page. The other 15 are
listed there as text-only links with no logo image ever uploaded, so
nothing could be sourced for them here — a logo for each would need to
come from that organization's own site:

- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
- Swedish Coast Guard
- Finnish Environment Institute (SYKE)
- The Maritime Office Gdynia
- Green Screen Festival
- Nature And Biodiversity Conservation Union (NABU)
- Ikanawtiket
- Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI)
- Atlantic Branch of P.P. Shirshov Institute of Oceanology, Russian Academy of Sciences
- Finland Ministry of the Environment
- Geological Survey of Sweden
- The Nature Research Centre
- Inspekcja Ochrony Środowiska (IOS)
- Gesellschaft zur Rettung der Delphine (GRD)
- Gesellschaft zum Schutz der Meeressäugetiere (GSM)
